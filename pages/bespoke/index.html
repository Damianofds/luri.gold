Bespoke

I love to develop the design of your dreams with you! Together we will create your very own, individual piece of jewellery. We will share our inspirations and ideas through descriptions, drawings and photos. As I will create it on the bench, I will keep you involved throughout the process to make sure that you will love your new jewel.

If you have any pieces of your own that you would like to be respectfully redesigned, together we will give them a new life. Read more about my heirloom transformation service.

Get inspired by my designs that you can find here or on my Instagram profile.

Pricing and conditions

Creating a jewel just for you is a process that requires a lot of time for communication, designing, sourcing the perfect gem and often experimentation of new methods. Thus, please consider an additional cost starting from 150 euros excluding all materials. For example if you love an already existing design, but would like to have it with another gemstone.

For a complete custom design the price will be according the complexity and we will agree on a range before starting. There is a deposit of about half price to be paid in advance that cannot be refunded. Bespoke jewellery is available in gold (alloys 750, 900 or 916) only. Please understand that custom pieces cannot be returned.

Please contact me for a phone or video consultation, I am so looking forward to meeting you!
